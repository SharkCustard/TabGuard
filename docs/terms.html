Tab Guard Terms of Use

Last updated: 16-10-2026

About Tab Guard

Tab Guard is a browser extension designed to detect and respond to suspicious tab-hijacking and tab-under behaviour. Features and behaviour may change as the extension is improved.

No guarantee

Tab Guard aims to reduce unwanted tab navigation, but no browser extension can guarantee detection or prevention of every unwanted, deceptive or malicious navigation.

User controls

Users remain responsible for their browsing decisions and may enable, disable or adjust Tab Guard's available protection and site controls.

Feedback

Users may provide feedback and diagnostics voluntarily. Diagnostic information is not uploaded automatically by Tab Guard.

Future Pro features

Some controls may be identified as planned Pro features. Where a feature is marked as coming soon, it is not currently offered for purchase.
