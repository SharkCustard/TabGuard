Tab Guard Closed Beta Terms

Draft for closed testing — review before public/commercial launch.

Beta software

Tab Guard BETA is pre-release software provided for testing and feedback. Features and behaviour may change, and the beta may contain defects.

Tester access

Beta Tester Passes are personal to the testing installation for which they are issued and may expire. Testers should not redistribute beta packages or passes without permission.

No guarantee

Tab Guard aims to detect suspicious tab behaviour, but no browser extension can guarantee detection or prevention of every unwanted navigation.

Feedback

Testers may provide feedback and diagnostics voluntarily. Diagnostics are not uploaded automatically by the current beta.
